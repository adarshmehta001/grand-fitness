## 3D Scene

The dumbbell 3D model in the homepage hero is constructed entirely from procedural geometry using [Three.js](https://threejs.org/) via [React Three Fiber](https://docs.pmnd.rs/react-three-fiber). No external 3D model files are used — no third-party assets or licenses apply to the 3D scene.
